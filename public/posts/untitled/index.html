Welcome!#

Hi, ’m Goblin Eater, welcome to my Cyber Cavern. I work in cybersecurity.

I have often thought about writing on the subject, but have never found the right tools to make it fluid for me. To be fair, I have a full-time job, a family, and am taking three classes a semester at the local community college. Yeah, I like being busy, but there comes a point when you have a few days between classes and its burning you up enough to make it happen. I’m at that point.

So, what is this about?#

This is about giving back. It’s about giving a hand up. Its about making cybersecurity more accessible.

I haven’t been in security (cybersecurity) all my career, but in the time I have spent in it I have seen big change. As I see the sands of time shift and move, it becomes obvious that the skills in the industry have to move too. They have to incorporate new technologies, new capabilities, a constantly changing attack surface, moving from physical, through virtual, to conceptual.

That mix of conditions makes it difficult for people doing the job, who are already stressed. They find it challenging to continue to be aware of the implication of these moves, to learn new methods. It makes it even harder for those looking to join the ranks. I want to help - I want to payback to the industry in the way that some very amazing folks have given to me.

What I can do is tell my story of career change, of connection, and of learning. I know I won’t be able to change everything, but if I can provide a different way of looking at what cybersecurity has to focus on, how to understand threats, weaknesses, and chains then I can maybe help more people cross the chasm of learning, to make it into the ranks of the cyber-warriors as valuable contributors, not just replacements to those burned out souls who were overwhelmed.

There is a fine line to trace here, and I recognize it well. There are practices, standards, processes, policies, and mandates to consider, and I am not railing against any of them. I am, however, determined to help accelerate the ramp to effectiveness and competence that is sorely needed.

Can I do it?#

I have no idea. But I know I can try.

Will I have wild ideas? For sure, but in the vein of rationalizing the issues more solidly. You never know, one of them might make a bit of sense. But I’ll only know if I share them and get them into the light.

So, as they say in some parts, “On with the show!”
